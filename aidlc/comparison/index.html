4 Organizational & Handshake Archetypes Across the 10 Frameworks

How teams organize humans + AI and pass work across roles and lifecycle phases:
1. Formal Mob & Guarded Gates
AWS AI-DLC · Anthropic SDLC
Streamlined PO + Devs + strict role separation; cryptographic/Git sign-offs govern phase handshakes.
2. Simulated Agile Studio
BMAD Method
Mirrors classic Scrum/Agile roles via 21+ AI personas passing sharded PRDs, Architecture, and Story files.
3. Automated Verifier & CI Loops
Cursor pstack · Tessl · Superpowers
Minimal manual phase gates; separate verifier subagents, TDD, or CI evals enforce handshakes automatically.
4. Flat Git & IDE Spec Teams
Spec Kit · OpenSpec · specs.md · Kiro Specs
No org restructuring; developers share lightweight Markdown specs/deltas through normal Git PRs & IDEs.

Quick Guide: Choosing by Organization Topology

Traditional PMs, Architects, Security & Devs needing clear accountability: Choose Anthropic AI-Native SDLC (async Git sign-offs + 5-role chain) or AWS AI-DLC (synchronous Mob workshops + compiled fences).
1–3 person team or solo builder wanting multi-role rigor: Choose BMAD Method (simulated PM, Architect, QA in terminal) or Superpowers (fresh subagents + 2-stage reviews).
Flat team of senior engineers hating process bottlenecks: Choose OpenSpec (clean living delta specs) or Cursor pstack (autonomous `/swarm` + `/arena` + `blast-radius`).
