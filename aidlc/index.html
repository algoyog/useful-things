30-Second Cheat Sheet: Which One Should You Pick?

Enterprise & Strict Governance
AWS AI-DLC (complex greenfield + DDD) or Anthropic AI-Native SDLC (separation of duties + intent.md without 33 stages).
Brownfield & Fast Iteration
OpenSpec (compact delta specs that never go stale) or specs.md FIRE (0–2 adaptive checkpoints on monorepos).
Speed, Portability or Living Specs
GitHub Spec Kit (clean portable standard), BMAD (agile AI personas), Cursor pstack (max-speed swarms), Tessl (spec-as-source).
